学术论文写作与规范Academic Writing & Research Ethics

GR601 · 研究生必修 · 2 学分 · 32 学时 Graduate (required) · 2 Credits · 32 Hours 产出：可投稿英文初稿Output: submission-ready draft

这是一门「动手写」的课。课程沿着一篇论文的真实产生过程推进：从检索文献、定位贡献、设计实验，到逐节写出摘要、引言、方法、结果和讨论，最后完成投稿信与审稿回应。课程结束时，每位同学应有一篇可以交给导师、并投向目标期刊或会议的英文初稿。 This is a hands-on writing course. It follows the real lifecycle of a paper: searching literature, positioning the contribution, designing experiments, then drafting abstract, introduction, method, results and discussion section by section, and finally writing a cover letter and a rebuttal. By the end, each student should hold an English draft ready to hand to their advisor and submit to a target venue.

课程简介Course Overview

这门课想带给你什么What this course aims to give you

很多研究生做得出好工作，却写不出配得上这份工作的论文。原因通常不是英语不好，而是没想清楚三件事：我的贡献到底是什么、它和已有工作的精确边界在哪里、以及读者为什么要相信我的结论。写作只是把这三件事想清楚的过程显现出来。 Many graduate students do good work but cannot write a paper that matches it. The obstacle is rarely English; it is usually three unclarified things: what exactly is my contribution, where precisely is its boundary against prior work, and why should a reader believe my conclusions. Writing simply makes the process of clarifying these three things visible.

因此这门课不是英语课，也不讲语法规则。我们逐节拆开一篇论文，讨论每一节要完成的说服任务：摘要要在 200 词内让人决定读下去；引言要立起「空白—问题—方案—证据」的链条；方法要让同行能复现；结果要让图表自己说话；讨论要诚实交代局限。每节课后你都会写一节，下一次课互相评。 So this is neither a language course nor a grammar class. We take a paper apart section by section and discuss the persuasive job of each: the abstract must make someone decide to read on within 200 words; the introduction must build the gap–problem–solution–evidence chain; the method must let a peer reproduce it; the results must let figures speak; the discussion must state limitations honestly. After each session you draft a section, and the next session peers review it.

课程同样花时间在不那么「技术」的部分：署名怎么排、什么算自我抄袭、审稿意见怎么回、被拒之后怎么办。这些事没人教，但几乎每个研究者都会在某一步上吃亏。 We also spend time on the less technical parts: authorship order, what counts as self-plagiarism, how to respond to reviewers, and what to do after a rejection. Nobody teaches these, yet almost every researcher gets burned at one of these steps.

学习目标Learning Outcomes

  1. 能够高效检索、筛选与管理文献，写出有观点而非罗列的文献综述。Search, filter and manage literature efficiently; write a survey with a thesis rather than a list.
  2. 能够用一句话说清自己工作的贡献，并将其放在与已有工作的精确边界上。State your contribution in one sentence and position it precisely against prior work.
  3. 能够设计支撑结论的实验，并让结果可复现、可核查。Design experiments that support the claim, with reproducible and checkable results.
  4. 能够独立完成一篇结构完整、语言达意、图表规范的英文论文初稿。Produce a complete English draft with sound structure, clear language and well-made figures.
  5. 能够撰写投稿信与审稿回应，并遵守署名、引用与学术伦理规范。Write cover letters and rebuttals, and follow authorship, citation and research-ethics norms.

课程政策Course Policies

互评是义务，不是可选项Peer Review Is Required

这门课的一半价值来自你收到的意见，另一半来自你给出的意见。未按时提交互评，本轮互评分记 0，且你将失去下一轮被评的资格。互评须具体：指出问题、说明原因、给出建议。Half the value of this course is the feedback you receive; the other half is the feedback you give. Missing a review deadline scores zero for that round and forfeits your place in the next round. Reviews must be specific: name the problem, explain why, suggest a fix.

学术诚信与自我抄袭Integrity & Self-Plagiarism

直接复制他人文字、图表或代码而未标注，本次作业记 0 分并按学校规定处理。复用你自己已发表的内容也须引用并说明，重复段落超过 15% 视为自我抄袭。所有作业将进行查重比对。Copying others' text, figures or code without attribution means a zero and a report per university rules. Reusing your own published material also requires citation and disclosure; over 15% overlap counts as self-plagiarism. All submissions are checked.

AI 使用规定AI Usage Policy

允许用 AI 润色英文表达、检查语法与生成图表代码，但不得让它生成论点、方法描述或实验结论。所有使用须在稿件末尾的「AI 使用声明」中写明工具、用途与提示词。未声明使用，或提交未经核验的 AI 生成内容，按学术诚信条款处理。You may use AI to polish English, check grammar and generate plotting code — not to produce arguments, method descriptions or conclusions. All use must be declared at the end of the manuscript with tool, purpose and prompts. Undeclared use, or submitting unverified AI-generated content, falls under the academic integrity clause.

署名与贡献声明Authorship

课程论文若基于实验室已有工作，须在提交时说明你的具体贡献以及合作者名单。涉及人类受试者、动物实验或敏感数据的研究，须附上伦理审查批件编号。If the paper builds on existing lab work, state your specific contribution and the collaborator list at submission. Research involving human subjects, animals or sensitive data must include the ethics approval number.

迟交与补交Late Submission

每迟交一天扣 10%，最多接受 3 天。期末初稿不接受迟交（需安排互评与答辩）。特殊情况请凭证明在截止前申请延期。10% per late day, up to 3 days. The final draft cannot be late (peer review and defense are scheduled). For special circumstances, apply before the deadline with documentation.

成绩申诉Grade Appeals

写作评分有主观成分，因此我接受申诉：请在公布后 7 天内邮件说明你的理由。我会重新阅读并书面回复；确有评分偏差会调整成绩。Grading writing involves judgment, so appeals are welcome: email your reasoning within 7 days of release. I will re-read and reply in writing; genuine errors will be corrected.

常见问题FAQ

我的研究还没做完，能写吗？My research isn't finished. Can I still write?

能，而且这正是最好的时机。写不下去的地方，往往就是研究还没想清楚的地方——这本身就是有用的诊断。课程要求的是「初稿」而非「可发表终稿」，重点是结构完整、论点清晰，缺的实验可以在方法节标注为待补。Yes — in fact that is the best time. Where writing stalls is usually where the thinking is still fuzzy, which is itself a useful diagnosis. The course asks for a draft, not a publishable final version: structure and clarity matter most, and missing experiments can be marked as pending in the method section.

我英语不好，会不会很吃力？My English is weak. Will I struggle?

会吃力，但这门课不按英语水平打分。评分看的是论证是否成立、结构是否清楚、证据是否支撑结论。语言表达的问题我会在批注里逐条标出，第 13 周专门讲常见中式表达的改法。往届英语最薄弱的几位同学，最后拿到的分数并不低。It will be harder, but this course does not grade on English proficiency. Grading looks at whether the argument holds, the structure is clear and the evidence supports the claim. Language issues are marked line by line in my comments, and Week 13 covers common Chinese-English patterns specifically. In past cohorts, the students with the weakest English did not end up with low grades.

我可以用 AI 润色吗？到哪里算越界？Can I use AI to polish? Where is the line?

改语法、润色句子、生成画图代码都可以；让 AI 写论点、写方法描述、编造实验结论不行。一个简单的判断标准：如果删掉 AI 的帮助，这段话里的想法是否仍然来自你？如果不是，就越界了。所有使用都要在文末声明。Grammar, sentence polishing and plotting code are fine; having AI produce arguments, method descriptions or fabricated conclusions is not. A simple test: if you removed the AI's help, would the ideas in the paragraph still be yours? If not, you have crossed the line. Declare all use at the end.

课程论文可以拿去投稿吗？Can I submit the course paper somewhere?

当然，这正是课程的目标。若你打算投稿，请在期末提交时说明目标 venue，我会在批注中按该 venue 的标准来要求。如果后来真的被接收，请告诉我一声——我会很高兴，也会在课堂上隐去你的信息后作为范例。Absolutely — that is the point. If you plan to submit, state your target venue with the final draft and I will hold your work to that venue's standard in my comments. If it later gets accepted, let me know: I will be delighted, and I may use it anonymously as a class example.

必须来上课吗？我是不是只要交作业就行？Do I have to attend? Can't I just submit the assignments?

不行。互评占 15% 且无法补做——你缺席就意味着另一位同学少收到一份意见，这会影响别人。出勤允许请假 2 次而不扣分；若确有长期冲突，请在第 1 周内邮件说明，我们会安排替代的互评任务。No. Peer review is 15% and cannot be made up — your absence means a classmate loses a review, which affects them. Two absences are excused without penalty. If you have a sustained conflict, email me during Week 1 and we will arrange an alternative review task.
