数据挖掘与知识发现Data Mining & Knowledge Discovery

CS405 · 本科四年级 / 研究生 · 3 学分 · 48 学时 Senior Undergraduate / Graduate · 3 Credits · 48 Hours

本课程围绕「从大规模数据中提炼可解释的知识」这一主线展开，覆盖数据预处理、关联分析、聚类、图挖掘与推荐系统五大模块。课程以真实数据集驱动：每学完一类方法，就在完整数据上跑通一遍，最终独立完成一份可交付的数据分析报告。 The course follows one thread — extracting interpretable knowledge from large-scale data — across five modules: preprocessing, association analysis, clustering, graph mining and recommender systems. It is dataset-driven: after each method you run it end-to-end on real data, finishing with a deliverable data-analysis report.

课程简介Course Overview

这门课想带给你什么What this course aims to give you

数据并不会自动说话。同样的交易日志，有人只看到一堆数字，有人能从中挖出稳定的购买模式——差别在于是否掌握一套系统化的挖掘方法。本课程的目标，就是让你成为后者。 Data does not speak by itself. Given the same transaction log, one person sees numbers, another extracts stable purchasing patterns — the difference is a systematic mining methodology. This course aims to make you the latter.

与《机器学习导论》侧重预测建模不同，本课程更关注「发现」：在没有明确标签的情况下理解数据结构，找出频繁模式、自然分组、关键节点与关联规律。因此课程会花相当篇幅讨论结果的可解释性——一个无法向业务方讲清楚的挖掘结果，价值接近于零。 Unlike Intro to ML, which emphasizes predictive modeling, this course focuses on discovery: understanding structure without labeled targets, finding frequent patterns, natural groupings, key nodes and associations. Considerable time goes to interpretability — a mining result you cannot explain to a stakeholder is worth close to zero.

学习目标Learning Outcomes

  1. 能够完成完整的数据挖掘流程：获取、清洗、探索、建模、评估与呈现。Carry out a full mining pipeline: acquisition, cleaning, exploration, modeling, evaluation and presentation.
  2. 能够正确选择并解释关联规则、聚类、图挖掘等无监督方法的适用条件与参数含义。Select and explain unsupervised methods — association rules, clustering, graph mining — including when they apply and what their parameters mean.
  3. 能够评估挖掘结果的质量与稳定性，识别偶然模式与虚假关联。Assess the quality and stability of mined results, spotting spurious patterns and false associations.
  4. 能够使用 NetworkX、scikit-learn、pandas 等工具在中等规模数据上完成端到端实验。Run end-to-end experiments on medium-scale data using NetworkX, scikit-learn and pandas.
  5. 能够撰写结构完整、结论可信、诚实说明局限的数据分析报告，并完成口头汇报。Write a well-structured, credible analysis report that states limitations honestly, and present it orally.

课程政策Course Policies

迟交与补交Late Submission

每迟交一天扣 10%，最多接受 3 天。期末报告不接受迟交（涉及答辩排期）。因病假、赛事等特殊情况，请凭证明在截止前申请延期。10% deducted per late day, up to 3 days. The final report cannot be late (defense scheduling). For illness or official events, request an extension before the deadline with documentation.

学术诚信Academic Integrity

允许讨论思路，代码与报告必须独立完成。直接复制他人作业或公开仓库代码而未标注，本次作业记 0 分并按学校规定处理。引用他人数据集、代码或图表必须明确标注来源。Discussing ideas is fine; code and reports must be your own. Copying others' work or public repo code without attribution results in a zero and a report per university rules. Cite all external datasets, code and figures.

生成式 AI 使用规定Generative AI Policy

允许使用 AI 辅助调试、润色英文表达与解释概念，但分析与结论必须源自你自己的实验。凡使用过 AI 工具，须在报告末尾「AI 使用声明」中说明用途与提示词。未声明而发现使用的，按学术诚信条款处理。You may use AI for debugging, polishing English and explaining concepts, but analysis and conclusions must come from your own experiments. Any use must be declared in the report's "AI usage statement" with purpose and prompts. Undeclared use falls under the academic integrity clause.

小组合作与分工Teamwork

期末项目 2–3 人一组，须提交每人贡献说明；组内互评会作为个人项目分的调整系数（0.8–1.2）。成员长期失联可申请组内调整，需在中期检查前提出。Projects are done in teams of 2–3 with a per-member contribution statement; peer evaluation scales individual project scores (0.8–1.2). Persistent non-participation can be reported for team adjustment before the mid-project check-in.

考勤与成绩申诉Attendance & Appeals

无故缺勤超过总课时 1/3 取消考试资格；请假需提前邮件告知助教。对成绩有异议，请在公布后 7 天内邮件提出，我会逐题复核。Missing over one third of classes without reason disqualifies you from the exam; email the TA in advance to request leave. Grade appeals must be raised by email within 7 days of release; I will re-check item by item.
