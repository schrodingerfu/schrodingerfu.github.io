人工智能通识AI Literacy

GE102 · 全校本科生 · 2 学分 · 32 学时 All Undergraduates · 2 Credits · 32 Hours 无需编程基础No coding required

这门课不教你写代码，而是教你在一个人人都能用 AI 的时代里保持清醒：AI 到底能做到什么、做不到什么，你的数据是怎样被使用的，以及哪些判断不能交给机器。课程以案例和讨论为主，涵盖数据素养、生成式 AI 的正确用法、算法偏见、深度伪造与治理规则。 This course does not teach you to code. It teaches you to stay clear-eyed in an age when everyone can use AI: what AI can and cannot do, how your data is used, and which judgments should never be delegated to a machine. Case-based and discussion-driven, it covers data literacy, responsible use of generative AI, algorithmic bias, deepfakes and governance.

课程简介Course Overview

这门课想带给你什么What this course aims to give you

生成式 AI 已经把「会用工具」的门槛降到几乎为零，但同时把「判断该不该用、用得对不对」的门槛抬高了。这正是这门课存在的理由——它关心的不是模型怎么训练，而是你作为使用者、被影响者和未来的决策者，该如何与之相处。 Generative AI has pushed the cost of "using the tool" to nearly zero, while raising the bar for judging whether and how it should be used. That is the reason this course exists: it is not about how models are trained, but about how you — as a user, someone affected, and a future decision-maker — should live with them.

课程分成三条线并行推进：一条是「能力线」，讲清 AI 的真实能力边界；一条是「数据线」，讲清你的数据如何被采集、推断和交易；一条是「社会线」，讨论偏见、深度伪造、职业冲击、版权与治理。每条线都从一个具体案例切入，而不是从概念开始。 Three threads run in parallel: a capability thread on what AI can actually do; a data thread on how your data is collected, inferred and traded; and a society thread on bias, deepfakes, labor impact, copyright and governance. Each starts from a concrete case rather than an abstract definition.

我唯一想让你带走的习惯是：面对任何 AI 输出，先问三句话——它基于什么数据？它错了会怎样？这个判断该由人来做吗？ The one habit I want you to leave with: for any AI output, ask three questions — what data is it based on? What happens if it is wrong? Should a human be making this call?

学习目标Learning Outcomes

  1. 能够用通俗语言解释机器学习与生成式 AI 的基本工作原理，并说清其能力边界。Explain in plain language how machine learning and generative AI work, and where their limits are.
  2. 能够判断一个具体任务是否适合交给 AI，并写出有效的提示词、核验输出结果。Decide whether a concrete task should be delegated to AI, write effective prompts, and verify outputs.
  3. 能够识别算法偏见、数据滥用与深度伪造的现实案例，并说明其社会后果。Recognize real cases of algorithmic bias, data misuse and deepfakes, and explain their social consequences.
  4. 能够读懂人工智能相关的法律法规与政策文件，了解自身的权利与义务。Read AI-related laws and policy documents, and understand your rights and obligations.
  5. 能够就一个有争议的 AI 议题形成有依据的观点，并清楚、诚实地表达出来。Form an evidence-based view on a contested AI issue and express it clearly and honestly.

AI 使用规定（本课特别说明）AI Usage Policy (course-specific)

这门课鼓励你使用 AI——要学会用它，就得真正用它。但你的观点、判断与结论必须来自你自己，且每次使用都要在文末「AI 使用声明」中写明用途、工具与提示词，并附上原始对话链接或截图。未声明使用，或提交未经你改动与核验的 AI 输出，按学术诚信条款处理。This course encourages AI use — you cannot learn to use a tool well without using it. But the opinions, judgments and conclusions must be yours, and every use must be declared at the end in an "AI usage statement" listing purpose, tool and prompts, with transcripts or screenshots attached. Undeclared use, or submitting unmodified unverified AI output, falls under the academic integrity clause.

常见问题FAQ

选课前最常被问到的几件事What students ask most before enrolling

我完全不会编程，也能选吗？I can't code at all. Can I still take this?

能，而且这门课就是为你设计的。全课程不写一行代码，不需要数学公式推导。课堂上的「实践」指的是用现成的 AI 工具完成任务、比较结果、判断可信度。Yes — this course is designed for you. There is no coding and no formula derivation. "Practice" here means using off-the-shelf AI tools to complete tasks, compare outputs and judge credibility.

用 AI 写作业算作弊吗？Is using AI on assignments cheating?

不算，但必须声明。这门课认为「会用 AI」是一项能力，所以鼓励使用；考核的是你的判断而不是 AI 的输出。规则很简单：写明用了什么、怎么用的、附原始对话，且最终内容经过你自己的修改与核验。偷偷用、或者原封不动提交，才构成问题。No, but you must declare it. This course treats "using AI well" as a skill, so it is encouraged; what we assess is your judgment, not the AI's output. The rule is simple: state what you used and how, attach the transcript, and make sure the final text is revised and verified by you. Using it covertly, or submitting output unchanged, is the problem.

我是文科生，会不会听不懂？I'm a humanities student — will I be lost?

不会。课程刻意从案例切入，第 6 周讲原理时也只用「预测下一个词」这类直觉解释。往届选课学生中文科生约占六成，期末通过率与理工科学生无显著差异。听不懂时请当场打断我——课堂上最受欢迎的问题就是「等等，这步为什么」。No. The course deliberately starts from cases, and even the Week 6 mechanics section relies on intuitions like "predicting the next word." Humanities students have made up about 60% of past cohorts, with pass rates comparable to STEM students. If something is unclear, interrupt me on the spot — "wait, why that step?" is the most welcome question in class.

上课要买教材吗？Do I need to buy the textbook?

不强制。《AI 3.0》为推荐主教材，图书馆有纸本与电子版，只读前两章即可跟上进度；其余阅读材料会全部发在 Blackboard 上，每单元 1–2 篇，加起来不超过 20 页。Not required. AI 3.0 is the recommended primary text and is available in print and electronic form at the library; the first two chapters suffice to keep up. All other readings are posted on Blackboard — 1–2 short pieces per unit, under 20 pages total.

出勤怎么算？晚上上课会有冲突吗？How is attendance counted? Will the evening slot clash?

出勤占 20%，允许请假 2 次且不扣分、无需证明。课程安排在周三晚 19:00–20:40，主要是为跨专业选课留出空间；若与必修课冲突，请在第 1 周内邮件告知，我们会说明补修方案。Attendance is 20%, with two excused absences requiring no documentation. The Wednesday 19:00–20:40 slot exists to leave room for cross-major enrollment. If it clashes with a required course, email me during Week 1 and we will explain the make-up options.

这门课和专业课（CS301/CS405）有什么区别？How is this different from CS301 / CS405?

CS301 与 CS405 面向计算机专业，教你建模与实现；本课程面向全校，教你判断与选择。前者输出的是模型，后者输出的是决策依据。如果你未来要做技术工作，推荐先上通识课建立整体判断，再进入专业课程。CS301 and CS405 are for CS majors and teach you to build and implement; this course is for the whole university and teaches you to judge and choose. The former produces models, the latter produces grounds for decisions. If you plan technical work, taking the literacy course first gives you the broader frame before specializing.
