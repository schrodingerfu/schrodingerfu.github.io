机器学习导论Introduction to Machine Learning

CS301 · 本科三年级 · 3 学分 · 64 学时 Junior Undergraduate · 3 Credits · 64 Hours

本课程系统讲授机器学习的核心思想、经典模型与工程实践方法。课程强调「从数学原理到可运行代码」的完整闭环：每个算法都会推导其优化目标，再动手实现并放到真实数据上验证。完成课程后，你将具备独立建模、评估与调优的能力。 This course covers the core ideas, classical models and engineering practice of machine learning. It emphasizes a full loop from mathematical principles to runnable code: for each algorithm we derive its objective, implement it, and validate it on real data. By the end, you will be able to build, evaluate and tune models independently.

课程简介Course Overview

这门课想带给你什么What this course aims to give you

机器学习已经从一门专门技术演变为理工科学生的通用素养。本课程不追求覆盖所有前沿模型，而是希望帮你建立一套稳定的判断力：拿到一个实际问题，知道该把它形式化成什么任务、该选什么基线模型、结果不好时该往哪个方向排查。 Machine learning has evolved from a specialty into general literacy for science and engineering students. Rather than surveying every frontier model, this course builds a stable sense of judgment: given a real problem, you will know how to formalize it, which baseline to pick, and where to look when results disappoint.

课程分为三个递进层次：前 8 周建立监督学习的完整方法论（建模、损失、优化、评估、正则化）；中段进入非线性模型与无监督学习；后段引入神经网络与实践专题，并以期末项目收尾。每周配有编程作业，全部在真实数据集上完成。 The course proceeds in three stages: the first eight weeks establish a complete methodology for supervised learning (modeling, loss, optimization, evaluation, regularization); the middle covers non-linear models and unsupervised learning; the final part introduces neural networks and practical topics, ending with a capstone project. Weekly programming assignments run on real datasets.

学习目标Learning Outcomes

  1. 能够把实际问题抽象为回归、分类、聚类等机器学习任务，并选择合适的评价标准。Formalize real problems as regression, classification or clustering tasks and choose appropriate evaluation metrics.
  2. 能够独立推导线性回归、逻辑回归、支持向量机等经典模型的优化目标与求解过程。Derive the objectives and solutions of linear regression, logistic regression and SVM independently.
  3. 能够用 NumPy / scikit-learn 从零实现并调试模型，理解每一行代码对应的数学含义。Implement and debug models with NumPy / scikit-learn from scratch, understanding the math behind each line.
  4. 能够诊断过拟合与欠拟合，运用交叉验证、正则化与超参搜索改进模型。Diagnose over- and under-fitting, and improve models with cross-validation, regularization and hyperparameter search.
  5. 能够规范地报告实验结果，诚实说明方法的局限，并具备阅读英文论文的能力。Report results rigorously, state limitations honestly, and read English research papers.

课程政策Course Policies

迟交与补交Late Submission

每迟交一天扣 10%，最多接受 3 天。因病假、赛事等特殊情况，可凭证明在截止前申请延期，另行安排不受扣分。10% deducted per late day, up to 3 days. For illness or official events, request an extension before the deadline with documentation — no penalty applies.

学术诚信Academic Integrity

允许讨论思路，但代码与报告必须独立完成。抄袭或复制他人作业，本次作业记 0 分并按学校规定处理；重复出现将直接判定课程不及格。Discussing ideas is fine; code and reports must be your own. Plagiarism results in a zero for that assignment and a report per university rules; repeat offenses mean failing the course.

生成式 AI 使用规定Generative AI Policy

允许使用 AI 辅助调试与解释概念，但不得直接提交 AI 生成的代码或文本。凡使用过 AI 工具，须在作业末尾的「AI 使用声明」中说明用途与提示词。未声明而发现使用的，按学术诚信条款处理。You may use AI to debug or explain concepts, but may not submit AI-generated code or text as your own. Any use must be declared in the "AI usage statement" at the end of the assignment, describing purpose and prompts. Undeclared use falls under the academic integrity clause.
