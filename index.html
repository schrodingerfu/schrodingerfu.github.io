Research & BackgroundResearch & Background

我于 2012 年在清华大学获得计算机科学与技术博士学位，2012—2015 年在新加坡国立大学从事博士后研究，2015 年加入江城大学，2019 年破格晋升教授。目前担任学院副院长、校学术委员会委员。 I received my PhD in Computer Science from Tsinghua University in 2012, worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the National University of Singapore (2012–2015), and joined Jiangcheng University in 2015, where I was promoted to full professor in 2019. I currently serve as vice dean of the school and a member of the university academic committee.

我的研究强调「方法可解释、结果可复现」。近年来主要关注模型在分布偏移与长尾数据下的可靠性问题，并尝试把因果推断引入推荐与决策系统。我也长期参与人工智能通识课程建设，希望让非计算机专业的学生同样具备数据与算法素养。 My research emphasizes interpretable methods and reproducible results. Recent work focuses on model reliability under distribution shift and long-tailed data, and introduces causal inference into recommendation and decision systems. I also contribute to AI general-education curricula, aiming to build data and algorithmic literacy for students outside computer science.

研究兴趣Research Interests

机器学习与表征学习Machine Learning & Representation Learning

面向开放环境的鲁棒表征、自监督预训练、分布外泛化。Robust representations for open environments, self-supervised pre-training, out-of-distribution generalization.

数据挖掘与推荐系统Data Mining & Recommender Systems

可解释推荐、因果推断与反事实评估、序列行为建模。Explainable recommendation, causal inference and counterfactual evaluation, sequential behavior modeling.

人工智能教育应用AI for Education

知识追踪、学习者画像、自适应练习与教学资源推荐。Knowledge tracing, learner profiling, adaptive exercise and resource recommendation.

科学文献智能理解Scientific Document Understanding

多模态大模型的文献解析、公式与图表抽取、学术知识图谱构建。Multimodal LLM-based document parsing, formula and figure extraction, academic knowledge graph construction.

个人爱好与兴趣Interests & Hobbies

研究之外，我是一个这样的人Beyond research, who I am

胶片摄影Film Photography

用一台 1985 年的手动胶片机记录校园四季，偏爱清晨与雨天的散射光。每年整理一册《校园光景》摄影集。Shooting campus seasons with a 1985 manual film camera; fond of early-morning and rainy-day light. I compile an annual photo book.

徒步与登山Hiking & Mountaineering

几乎每个假期都在山里。已完成多条长线徒步路线，习惯在行走中构思论文框架——很多想法诞生于海拔两千米以上。I spend most holidays in the mountains and have finished several long-distance trails. Many paper outlines were conceived above 2,000 meters.

古典音乐与黑胶收藏Classical Music & Vinyl

收藏约 400 张黑胶唱片，偏爱巴赫与马勒。写作时习惯放协奏曲，改论文时放无伴奏大提琴。About 400 vinyl records, with a preference for Bach and Mahler. Concertos for writing; solo cello for revising papers.

科普写作与书法Popular Science & Calligraphy

业余为科普专栏撰稿，尝试把机器学习讲给中学生听；每天临帖半小时，用来对抗屏幕时间。I write popular-science columns explaining machine learning to middle-schoolers, and practice calligraphy half an hour daily to offset screen time.

招生说明Prospective Students

每年招收硕士生 2–3 名、博士生 1 名，同时常年接收本科生参与科研训练。希望你具备扎实的数学基础、良好的编程能力，以及对问题本身的好奇心——这比履历上的任何一项都重要。I recruit 2–3 master's and 1 PhD student per year, and welcome undergraduates for research training year-round. Solid math, decent coding and genuine curiosity about the problem matter more than anything on a CV.

联系时请附上：个人简历、成绩单，以及一段说明你为什么对这个方向感兴趣的简短陈述（不超过一页）。When contacting me, please attach your CV, transcript, and a one-page statement on why this direction interests you.
